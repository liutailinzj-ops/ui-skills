# Asset Policy

## Customer assets

Use customer-provided assets when their identity and intended placement are clear. Preserve replaceability and original aspect ratio where appropriate.

## Missing assets

Apply the workflow placeholder policy. Use generated or licensed placeholders when available; otherwise use neutral image frames with explicit labels.

## Competitor/reference assets

- Use only for internal composition reference.
- Keep captures on `90_References`.
- Do not imply customer ownership or approval.
- Do not send competitor assets to production.
- Prefer generated or licensed placeholder images in customer-facing review frames.

## Source tracking

Record each non-customer asset as customer, licensed, generated, competitor-reference, or temporary-unknown in the manifest.
