# DTC and B2B Routing

## DTC

Prioritize the combination appropriate to the product:

- Product comprehension and differentiation.
- Brand expression.
- Visual desire and use context.
- Proof, trust, objections, purchase confidence, and repeat purchase.
- Product discovery and comparison where the catalog requires it.

## B2B

Prioritize the combination appropriate to the buying process:

- Product/category navigation.
- Applications and use cases.
- Technical specifications and documentation.
- Certifications, capabilities, cases, and operational proof.
- Contact, RFQ, distributor, sample, or sales-assistance paths.

Do not invent proof. Create the structure and use explicit placeholders until customer facts arrive.
