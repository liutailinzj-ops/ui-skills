# DTC and B2B Routing

## DTC

Build from the Product and Competitor Analysis. Prioritize the combination appropriate to the product:

- Product comprehension and differentiation.
- Brand expression.
- Visual desire and use context.
- Proof, trust, objections, purchase confidence, and repeat purchase.
- Product discovery and comparison where the catalog requires it.
- A traceable conversion chain from entry intent through comprehension, relevance, differentiation, proof, evaluation, objection handling, action, and retention where applicable.

Do not turn the conversion chain into a fixed module sequence. Allocate responsibilities across the requested page and connected pages according to visitor intent, product complexity, traffic source, and available evidence.

## B2B

Prioritize the combination appropriate to the buying process:

- Product/category navigation.
- Applications and use cases.
- Technical specifications and documentation.
- Certifications, capabilities, cases, and operational proof.
- Contact, RFQ, distributor, sample, or sales-assistance paths.

Do not invent proof. Create the structure and use explicit placeholders until customer facts arrive.

B2B is a stable baseline plus industry extensions, not a single universal sitemap. Add application, solution, technical, resource, distributor, service, or industry pages only when the buying path and project scope support them.
